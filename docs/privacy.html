Privacy policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

Timelime is a local-first app. Your plan is stored on your Mac, and it is only ever sent anywhere if you choose to sign in.

If you never sign in

Nothing is collected and nothing leaves your computer. The app stores your plan on your own disk and works with no account and no network connection. The only request it makes is to GitHub, to see whether a newer version has been released.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and exists so your plan can reach your other Macs and survive the loss of this one. Two things are then stored:

  • Your Google account details - your email address, name, profile picture and Google account ID. These identify your plan as yours and are shown in the app so you can see who is signed in.
  • Your plan - the blocks, lanes and dependencies you create, along with a timestamp per record so edits from two Macs can be merged. It is held as a single document readable only by your own account.

Both are stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes this data as the infrastructure provider; their handling is covered by the Firebase privacy documentation.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, usage tracking or crash reporting.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to your files, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Sign-in happens in your browser

The app never sees your Google password. Sign-in opens your normal browser, Google authenticates you there, and only a token identifying you is returned to the app.

Keeping your data, and deleting it

Your plan is kept for as long as your account exists. You can sign out at any time from the app's settings menu, which stops all syncing and leaves your plan on your Mac untouched.

To delete the copy held in the cloud, along with your account details, email REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL and it will be removed. Deleting the cloud copy does not remove the plan from your own Mac; you can export it first from the settings menu.

Children

Timelime is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
